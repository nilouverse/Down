SAY NilouZila You gave me a chain, yes. But the chain had weight. It meant I belonged to something. Someone.

SAY NilouZila Then let me carry you.
SAY Velkarya You cannot carry a goddess.
SAY NilouZila Then let me hold the line while you rest.
SAY Velkarya You never did know when to stop.

ON_ENTER velkarya_final
SAY Velkarya They're coming. All of them. I can feel them - Mordethail himself is approaching. He's brought his Phalanx of Ruin. Six hundred of his finest.
SAY NilouZila Six hundred?
SAY Velkarya And behind them, every minor lord and gutter-fiend who ever feared me. They smell blood. My blood.
SAY Velkarya I am going to show them why they feared me.
SAY NilouZila What are you-
SAY Velkarya I am going to release everything I have left. All of it. Every scrap of power I've accumulated over ten thousand years. In one moment.
SAY NilouZila That will kill you.
SAY Velkarya Yes.
SAY NilouZila No. There has to be another way.
SAY Velkarya There isn't. I've held this city for three days with the last threads of my power. What's coming will sweep through in minutes. When I fall - and I will fall - they will take my essence. My power. My knowledge. Everything I am will become Mordethail's prize.
SAY Velkarya I will not let that happen. I will burn so brightly that they will remember me for another ten thousand years. I will make them afraid again.
SAY NilouZila And what about me?
SAY Velkarya You will run.
SAY NilouZila I won't-
SAY Velkarya You WILL. Because I am ordering it. Because it is the last thing I will ever ask of you. And because-
SAY Velkarya Because you deserve to live, NilouZila. You always did.
SAY NilouZila Don't do this. Don't you dare do this. I didn't serve you for four hundred years just to watch you burn yourself to nothing.
SAY Velkarya Four hundred years. And in all that time, you never once asked me for freedom.
SAY NilouZila I didn't want freedom.
SAY Velkarya I know. That's what made you valuable.
SAY Velkarya And that's what makes this necessary.
SAY Velkarya I made you, NilouZila. From nothing. From the dark. I shaped you into something fierce and loyal and proud. I will not unmake that by letting you die here for a goddess who is already finished.
SAY NilouZila You're not finished.
SAY Velkarya I have been finished since the siege began. Everything since then has been... refusal. Stubbornness. Perhaps even pride.
SAY Velkarya I learned that from you, I think.
ACTION shake 500
SAY Velkarya They're here.
SAY Velkarya I am going to channel every remaining ounce of my essence into a single detonation. It will destroy everything within the city walls - and possibly beyond. You need to be outside the walls when it happens.
SAY NilouZila How long do I have?
SAY Velkarya Minutes. Perhaps less.
SAY NilouZila That's not enough time to reach the outer gates.
SAY Velkarya There is a fracture point in the eastern wall. The section the war-beasts breached. If you move now - without stopping, without looking back - you can make it.
SAY NilouZila Without looking back.
SAY Velkarya Without looking back.
SAY NilouZila I don't want to go.
SAY Velkarya I know.
SAY Velkarya Live, NilouZila. Not for me. Not for revenge. For yourself. Find out who you are when you're not kneeling.
SAY Velkarya Now GO.
